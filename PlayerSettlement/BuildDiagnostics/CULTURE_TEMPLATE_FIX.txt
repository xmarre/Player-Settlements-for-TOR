7.6.3 culture-template hotfix

Symptom: selected towns/castles used the Wood Elf visual.
Trigger: selected CultureObject.StringId or requested settlement type had no exact template.
Root cause: the fallback list began with battania and UpdateSettlementVisualEntity clamped the invalid start index to zero.
Invariant restored: every TOR 1.16 main culture has at least one Town and one Castle template, so selection resolves within the chosen culture instead of entering the global fallback.
