Player Settlements 7.6.8 runtime acceptance tests

1. Existing-save visual reconstruction
- Load a save containing generated TOR villages, castles and towns.
- Confirm every generated settlement keeps its campaign-map icon/model.
- Pan and time-accelerate near the settlements for several in-game hours.

2. Village raid lifecycle
- Allow a hostile lord to start raiding a generated village.
- Confirm the game remains stable at raid start, during raid progress, on interruption and on completion.
- Save and reload while the village is being raided, then repeat the map-visual check.

3. Existing fortification siege eligibility
- Load an existing save while at war with a kingdom capable of fielding an army.
- Confirm hostile parties/armies select a generated player town or castle and begin a real siege instead of approaching and abandoning the target.
- Confirm the siege camp and siege event remain active for at least one campaign day.

4. Newly built fortification siege eligibility
- Build one new custom castle and one new custom town.
- Confirm both appear under the player clan's fiefs immediately without reloading.
- Start/continue a war and confirm both can become native military siege targets.

5. Ownership preservation
- Transfer or lose a generated fortification to another clan, save, and reload.
- Confirm the 7.6.8 session repair does not return it to Clan.PlayerClan.

6. Regression checks
- Enter generated settlements and load their scenes.
- Confirm bound villages, trade-bound towns, prosperity, garrison and settlement menus remain functional.
- Confirm no recurring campaign-map performance cost; the repair runs only on session launch.
