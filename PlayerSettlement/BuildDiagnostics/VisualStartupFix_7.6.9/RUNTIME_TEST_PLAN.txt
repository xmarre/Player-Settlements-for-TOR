Player Settlement 7.6.9 runtime test plan
==========================================

A. Startup regression
1. Clean-install 7.6.9.
2. Load the same save used for the 7.6.8.2 diagnostic reproduction.
3. Wait for all settlement visuals to appear.
4. Confirm no red NullReferenceException messages occur.
5. Exit and reload the save once.

B. Visual identity
1. Inspect existing custom village, castle, and town icons.
2. Create one settlement with an Eonir/ToR template.
3. Confirm its preview and persistent map visual retain the selected culture.
4. Confirm a failed prefab lookup does not cycle into another culture.

C. Village raid
1. Let an enemy begin raiding a custom village.
2. Observe the raid for several campaign hours.
3. Interrupt one raid.
4. Allow another raid to complete.
5. Save/reload while a raid is active.
6. Confirm no TickVisuals access violation or missing settlement visual.

D. Siege AI
1. Use a save where a hostile kingdom has sufficient strength and proximity.
2. Confirm a custom town/castle can become an army objective.
3. Confirm the army begins and maintains a real siege.
4. Repeat for a newly built custom fortification.
5. Account for ordinary native AI target-selection factors.

E. Ownership preservation
1. Transfer or lose a custom fortification to another clan.
2. Save and reload.
3. Confirm the settlement remains with that clan.
