Player Settlement 7.6.9 root cause and validation
==================================================

Observed runtime stack
----------------------
System.NullReferenceException
  at BannerlordPlayerSettlement.Patches.PartyVisualPatch.OnStartup(
      SettlementVisual& __instance,
      Dictionary<int,List<GameEntity>>& _gateBannerEntitiesWithLevels)

followed immediately by:

System.NullReferenceException
  at SandBox.View.Map.Visuals.SettlementVisual.OnStartup_Patch1(
      SettlementVisual this)

The pair occurred twice, accounting for all four red startup messages.

Interpretation
--------------
The custom prefix reached a null-dependent path while initializing a generated
settlement visual. Its broad catch logged the exception and then returned true.
Harmony therefore continued into Bannerlord's original SettlementVisual.OnStartup
with the same incomplete visual state, producing the paired second exception.

Corrected invariants
--------------------
- Non-custom settlements always return to native startup untouched.
- Custom/overwritten settlements use the Player Settlement startup path.
- Once a custom visual enters that path, a failure cannot fall through into the
  native startup method with incomplete state.
- A missing strategic entity is handled without dereferencing it.
- ToR generated settlement ids use an independent same-culture visual copy.
- Map banner placeholders may have no parent.
- Siege camp frame collections may be absent.
- Reflection accessors and map visual dictionaries may be unavailable.
- Saved deep-edit indices may no longer match the current visual hierarchy.
- A failed preview cannot advance to a different culture as a fallback.

Preserved behavior
------------------
- Placement-tick-owned gate and port commits.
- Save-and-continue without an in-process campaign reload.
- Exact-culture ToR settlement visuals.
- Independent prefab-backed copied map entities.
- Native owner transition for new player towns/castles.
- Existing-save raid and ownership repair.
- No campaign-map tick scan.
